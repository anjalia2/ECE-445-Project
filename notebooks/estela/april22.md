ESP32 broke 🙁 I had to re-solder it. Surprisingly, I think what broke was the C2102 IC. When desoldered, 5V and 3.3V stopped shorting. Replaced the ESP32 too since we were unable to re-program it after the incident.

Couple of notes to keep in mind for next time:

- Always buy at least 1.5 times more the amount of components you need per board ordered. So, if you order 5 boards, order components for at least 7.
- Make sure your budget allows for it!
- When soldering the bottom GND pad for the ESP32, use a heat gun from the bottom of the board and use solder paste! Clean area beforehand.
- Always check connections before plugging in to power.
